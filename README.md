# 🌟 R.S. Mangalam Digital Portal & Civic Hub

Welcome to the official repository for the **R.S. Mangalam Digital Portal** (`rsmangalam-portal`), a community digital hub and environmental monitoring web platform created for **R.S. Mangalam (Pincode: 623525), Ramanathapuram District, Tamil Nadu**.

This platform provides live weather and air quality metrics, local Vetri Payanam bus timings, a civic digital directory, and acts as the official digital showcase for **Rainbow Readymades & Computer Academy**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- 🌤️ **Live Environmental Dashboard:** Real-time data fetching via Open-Meteo API for R.S. Mangalam ($9.8242^\circ\text{N}, 78.6653^\circ\text{E}$):
  - Temperature & "Feels Like" metrics
  - Live US Air Quality Index (AQI) with PM2.5, PM10, NO2, and SO2 breakdown
  - Sunlight UV Index, Sunrise, and Sunset times
  - Humidity, Dew Point, Atmospheric Pressure, and Rain Depth
  - Interactive 24-Hour Forecast Chart powered by Chart.js
- 🚌 **Vetri Payanam Bus Schedules:** Searchable timing list for local transit covering Ramanathapuram, Madurai, Karaikudi, Paramakudi, and Chennai routes.
- 📞 **Civic Digital Directory:** Emergency services, Taluk Office, EB Office, Police Station, Healthcare centers, and local utility contacts with direct click-to-call functionality.
- 🏬 **Rainbow Business Hub:** Featured course offerings, retail fashion collections, special enrollment forms, and business updates.
- 📱 **Mobile-First & Modern UI:** Built with Tailwind CSS, Glassmorphism aesthetic, automatic theme switching (Dark/Light mode), dynamic search filters, and sticky mobile navigation.
